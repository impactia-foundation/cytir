Limited medical use of otherwise prohibited narcotics

This synthetic page describes how the competent authority may grant an exceptional authorisation for the limited medical use of otherwise prohibited narcotics, such as psilocybin and MDMA, for patients with treatment-resistant conditions.

An application must name the treating physician, the patient group, the substance, the dose range and the monitoring plan. The authority decides each application individually and may attach conditions, including reporting of adverse events within the stated deadline.

Procedure

The physician submits the application form together with a written justification. The authority acknowledges receipt, may request further information, and issues a decision in writing. A positive decision is valid for a limited period and may be renewed on request.
